## Project Log 1:
### 1. The Primary Driver

The primary driver behind my project is pursuing my own ideas. When I see a better way for something to be done, I like to pursue it and make it real. While being my own boss and the potential for financial rewards are also nice aspects of entrepreneurship, I feel that exploring and developing my ideas gives me more fulfillment than the other two.

### 2. The Value Hypothesis

The primary problem this software hopes to address is that effectively planning and managing time can be unnecessarily difficult and time consuming. Many people have multiple responsibilities and goals but don't have a simple way to turn them into a schedule they can actually follow. The application will aim to  make the process of scheduling easier by gamifying the experience and streamlining it. By making scheduling simpler that can help people use their limited time more intentionally.

### 3. The Intensity Challenge

The entrepreneurial intensity trait I expect to be the biggest challenge for this project is **innovation**. I have a specific vision for how I want the scheduling experience to work which could lead me to spend too much time refining features and trying to make the product match that vision perfectly. During the 14 week timeframe, I will need to balance pursuing the details with the practical need to build, test, and release a functional product.
